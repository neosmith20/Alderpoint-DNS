Alderpoint DNS is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial

rights.

## What requires a separate license

Under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, the following (this list is
illustrative, not exhaustive) require a separate written license from the
copyright holder:

- Selling Alderpoint DNS, or software that includes it.
- Bundling Alderpoint DNS into a paid product.
- Offering Alderpoint DNS as a paid hosted or managed service.
- Using Alderpoint DNS as a component of a paid managed service, even if
  Alderpoint DNS itself is not the product being sold.
- Commercially redistributing Alderpoint DNS, modified or unmodified.

If you are unsure whether your intended use requires a commercial license,
ask before proceeding — see "Contact" below.

## How commercial licensing works

The copyright holder may grant, deny, or negotiate commercial licenses for
Alderpoint DNS independently, on a case-by-case basis. There is no
standing commercial license offer, no published pricing, and no guarantee
that a commercial license will be granted for any particular request.

This document does not itself grant, promise, or imply any commercial
rights. It exists only to explain that commercial use requires a separate
agreement and how to start that conversation.

## Contact

Commercial licensing inquiries should be made through the Alderpoint DNS
GitHub repository (an issue or discussion, as appropriate) until a
dedicated commercial contact method is published here.
