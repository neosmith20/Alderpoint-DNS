Pi-hole Migration

Upload a Pi-hole text/list export -- adlists.list (blocklist subscription URLs, one per line), whitelist.txt / blacklist.txt (exact-match allow/block domains), regex.list (regex allow/block rules, including the (\.|^)domain.tld$ wildcard-block idiom), custom.list (hosts-style entries, imported as Local DNS A/AAAA records), and dnsmasq cname=alias,target[,ttl] rewrite lines. Section headers such as [whitelist] or [regex_black] switch how bare lines are read; keyword-prefixed lines (whitelist, blacklist, regex, …) work anywhere in the file.

Pi-hole group assignments and per-client scoping have no Alderpoint DNS equivalent -- they are never silently dropped, but reported as explicit unsupported/client-scoped findings in the preview instead. Gravity database internals are not read directly; export one of the practical text files above from Pi-hole first.
